Study Title:

Effects of Performance Pressure and Task Difficulty on Problem-Solving Performance

Principal Investigator:

Aidan Barry - Psych 402 Experimental Research, Saint Mary's College of California

Purpose of the Study:

This study is investigating how performance pressure and task difficulty influence problem-solving ability using the Tower of Hanoi puzzle.

Procedures:

You will complete the Tower of Hanoi puzzle (estimated time: 1-6 minutes). Your completion time and number of moves will be automatically recorded.

Risks and Discomforts:

Minimal risks — possible mild frustration.

Confidentiality & Voluntary Participation:

Participation is voluntary and anonymous. You may withdraw at any time.

Debriefing Information

This study examined the effects of performance pressure and task difficulty on problem-solving performance.

Manipulations:
