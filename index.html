Debriefing Information

Thank you for your participation in this study. The purpose of this research was to examine the effects of performance pressure and task difficulty on problem-solving performance using the Tower of Hanoi puzzle.

Manipulations Used:

  • Performance Pressure: Participants were randomly assigned to either a neutral condition or a pressure condition. The pressure condition included the prompt: “This is a test of intelligence. Many people struggle with this task...”
  • Task Difficulty: Participants were randomly assigned to either a 3-block (easier) or 4-block (harder) version.

Hypotheses:
We predicted that pressure would increase completion time, especially on the more difficult (4-block) version.

This study was inspired by previous research including Baumeister (1984) and Beilock & Carr (2005).

Your data has been recorded anonymously. If you wish to withdraw your data, please contact Aidan Barry at adb15@stmarys-ca.edu.
