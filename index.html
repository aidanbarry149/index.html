Informed Consent

Study Title:

Effects of Performance Pressure and Task Difficulty on Problem-Solving

Principal Investigator:

Aidan Barry - Psych 402 Experimental Research, Saint Mary's College of California

Purpose:

This study investigates how performance pressure and task difficulty affect problem-solving ability using the Tower of Hanoi puzzle.

Procedures:

You will complete a short puzzle (estimated time: 1-6 minutes). Your completion time and number of moves will be recorded automatically. The entire study should take no more than 10 minutes.

Risks and Benefits:

Risks are minimal and similar to solving any online puzzle (possible mild frustration). There are no direct personal benefits, but your participation will help advance psychological research.

Confidentiality & Voluntary Participation:

Participation is completely voluntary and anonymous. No identifying information is collected. You may withdraw at any time without penalty by closing the browser.

Instructions

Solve the Tower of Hanoi

Moves: 0

Debriefing

This study examined the effects of performance pressure and task difficulty on problem-solving performance using the Tower of Hanoi puzzle.

What we manipulated:

  • Performance Pressure: Some participants saw a high-pressure prompt ("This is a test of intelligence..."), while others saw neutral instructions.
  • Task Difficulty: You were randomly assigned to either a 3-disk (easier) or 4-disk (harder) version.

Your completion time and number of moves have been recorded anonymously.
